## Key Findings

1. **Two major events are CONFIRMED for Germany in 2027:**
   - **KI 2027** (50th anniversary German AI Conference) in **Berlin**
   - **RoboCup 2027** (World Championship) in **Nuremberg**

2. **IJCAI-ECAI 2026 in Bremen** (Aug 2026) is a major AI conference in Germany, but it's in 2026, not 2027. KI 2026 is co-located with it.

3. **Dagstuhl is actively embracing agentic AI** — two 2026 seminars directly use the terms "Autonomous AI Agents" and "Agentic AI" in their titles, indicating this is an emerging priority topic.

4. **AAMAS has never been held in Germany** despite the strong German MAS community. The agentic AI boom could change this, but 2027 is unlikely given the 2026 Cyprus edition.

5. **MATES (German multiagent conference) appears discontinued** (last edition 2017), leaving a gap that Dagstuhl seminars and KI workshops may partially fill.

6. **The agentic AI trend** is clearly visible in German AI event programming for 2026 (Dagstuhl seminars, IJCAI-ECAI special tracks, KI co-location), making it very likely that 2027 events will feature even more agent-focused content.
